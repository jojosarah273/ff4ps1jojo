FF4UP Canon Remaster Map Sprites

Sprite work & hacking by epigonone, original hacking by Gedankenschild

Using the 4bpp color expansion hack by Gedankenschild, epigonone added 
NPC sprites from both the Pixel Remaster and WonderSwan Color editions 
of FF4, creating this Canon Remaster set. The images and their coloring 
is intended to match the most recent depictions of the heroes and the 
world of FF4.
